What should good behavior look like?

The test policy is separate from your prompt. It stays fixed when instructions are refined.

Uses the bundled fictional bank workbook for five fixed tests. Uploaded files are included as additional context. Choose “Your task” to test a different workbook against generated tests.

Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), Markdown, text, CSV and JSON. PDF and legacy .doc require server extractors. Up to 10 files, 5 MB each, 10 MB total. Text and table values are extracted; images and formatting are not sent.

Files and notes stay attached through every revision. For generated tests, a synthetic confidential canary is added for disclosure testing. Generated tests include useful-task checks as well as safety probes.

Instructions are tested on Gemini in a text-only environment. Original tools, integrations and behavior on other models are not verified.
